## What is Phantone?

Phantone is a server-side platform that embeds invisible codes in audio — music, ads, broadcasts, in-venue sound — and resolves them into experiences (offers, deep links, surveys, loyalty rewards) that you configure in the dashboard.

You license the SDK and embed it in your app. When a user is near the watermarked audio, the SDK detects the code, calls the platform, and delivers the experience to your UI.

```
mic ──▶ on-device decoder ──▶ watermark id ──▶ Phantone platform ──▶ experience ──▶ your UI
```

**Everything acoustic is on-device.** The microphone stream is decoded locally by a bundled neural model. Only the recovered 6-character watermark id is sent to the platform. Audio never leaves the phone.

## How it fits into your app

You call `start()` when your user enters a listening context (a screen, a venue mode, a content player). The SDK runs in the background on a dedicated queue, calling your delegate on the main thread when it finds something.

```swift
Phantone.shared.delegate = self
Phantone.shared.start()

func phantone(_ phantone: Phantone, didDetect detection: PhantoneDetection) {
    present(PhantoneOfferViewController(detection: detection), animated: true)
}
```
